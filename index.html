House rules

Four promises in every cup & bowl.

No fine print and no exceptions. From a ₹59 juice to a loaded salad, everything is made the same honest way.

  • Zero added sugar

    Sweetness comes from the fruit. We never add sugar.

  • Olive oil only

    Olive oil is the only oil we cook and dress with.

  • No palm oil

    Not in our food. Not in anything we make.

  • Air frying only

    Anything crisp is air fried, never deep fried.
